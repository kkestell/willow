# Lexer review

## Scope and coverage

Reviewed the staged Lexer changes against `agents/plans/2026-10-14-002-lexer.md`, `willow.md` §3, §4, §10, and §11, and `AGENTS.md`: `compiler/lexer.lua`, `tests/unit/lexer_test.lua`, `tests/programs/unknown_escape.wlo` and `.err`, and `agents/todo.md`. Also read the code the lexer depends on or feeds (`compiler/span.lua`, `compiler/diag.lua`, `compiler/cli.lua`, `tests/run.lua`). The parser and later passes are stubs, so valid files end in an internal compiler error at the parser; how the parser consumes tokens, including EOF inside open parentheses and a stray `)`, is outside this review. Checks ran on macOS only.

The implementation lexes physical lines in an outer loop with `lex_line` and `read_string` helpers rather than one flat loop, and the paren depth counter stays at zero on a stray `)`. Both produce the token stream the plan specifies.
