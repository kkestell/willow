## Summary

The four blocking TCP operations in `runtime/host.lua` now take the function
they wait with as their first parameter: `__wlo_socket_connect`,
`__wlo_socket_recv`, `__wlo_socket_sendall`, and `__wlo_socket_accept`. The
`net` helpers keep their signatures and pass `__wlo_wait_fd`, so their
behavior is unchanged. A later slice passes a suspending wait from
`runtime/aio.lua` through `__wlo_runtime.socket_*`.
